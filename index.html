Prototype · synthetic data · AI simulated

One data trail, five people helped

Teachers already record attendance, syllabus coverage and course-outcome (CO) marks in GPA Care. Kaushal Setu turns that same data into help for each person around the student, with no extra data entry.

Every AI output in this prototype is simulated with fixed rules and sample content. In the real system, AI answers only from teacher-approved syllabus content, and a teacher approves what goes to students.

2-minute demo path

    Udyog Setu — verified local talent for employers

    An employer describes the need in plain Hindi or English. AI turns it into course outcomes, then matches students whose skills are verified by classroom evidence and who have consented to be shown.

    Matches Verified skills only

    Write a need and press Find.

    Local employers post small real problems. Students solve them as final-year projects, and the work is added to their Kaushal Passport as industry evidence.

    Kaushal Passport

    The QR holds only a signed link. Scanning it opens the college's verify page, which shows the record from the college, not from the PDF. Editing the PDF changes nothing. Try it with your phone camera.
